\chapter*{How to Use These Notes}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to Use These Notes}

These notes are a self-contained derivation-first reference.
Every section follows the same contract:
\begin{itemize}
  \item One motivating sentence before every definition.
  \item Every result that matters is derived completely or the proof is cited explicitly.
  \item Boxed environments guide reading (see legend below).
\end{itemize}

\bigskip
\begin{description}[leftmargin=7em, labelwidth=6.5em, font=\normalfont]
  \item[\colorbox{roadmapBg}{\textcolor{roadmapTeal}{\textbf{Roadmap}}}]
        What we build and why -- read this first.
  \item[\colorbox{keyideaBg}{\textcolor{keyideaBlue}{\textbf{Key Idea}}}]
        Central conceptual insight.
  \item[\colorbox{keyeqnBg}{\textcolor{keyeqnYellow}{\textbf{Key Eqn}}}]
        An equation worth having at your fingertips.
  \item[\colorbox{intuitionBg}{\textcolor{intuitionGray}{\textbf{Intuition}}}]
        Concrete analogy or geometric picture.
  \item[\colorbox{mistakeBg}{\textcolor{mistakePink}{\textbf{Mistake}}}]
        A frequent misconception.
  \item[\colorbox{checkBg}{\textcolor{checkAmber}{\textbf{Check}}}]
        Inline verification: pause and confirm you followed the step.
  \item[\colorbox{refnoteBg}{\textcolor{refnoteViolet}{\textbf{Ref Note}}}]
        Pointers to sources for deeper treatment.
  \item[\colorbox{connectionBg}{\textcolor{connectionNavy}{\textbf{Connection}}}]
        Link to the target application domain.
\end{description}
